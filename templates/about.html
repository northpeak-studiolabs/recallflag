About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} collects official U.S. recall data every day and turns it into fast, searchable pages — one per recall, plus lists by brand, category, hazard, state, month and vehicle model.

Where the data comes from

  • FDA — food, drug and medical device enforcement reports via openFDA ({{ '{:,}'.format(counts.get('fda-food',0)) }} food, {{ '{:,}'.format(counts.get('fda-drug',0)) }} drug, {{ '{:,}'.format(counts.get('fda-device',0)) }} device recalls).
  • CPSC — consumer product recalls via the SaferProducts.gov Recalls API ({{ '{:,}'.format(counts.get('cpsc',0)) }}).
  • NHTSA — vehicle, equipment, tire and child-seat recalls from data.transportation.gov and NHTSA's recall flat files ({{ '{:,}'.format(counts.get('nhtsa',0)) }}).

The site covers recalls published since {{ since | human_date }}.

Limitations

  • FDA enforcement reports are published weekly and can lag the company's own announcement by days or weeks.
  • Hazard tags, product types and states are assigned automatically from the agencies' text and can be wrong or incomplete.
  • Vehicle recall pages list models and years named in the recall; not every vehicle is affected. Always check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.

Independence

{{ site_name }} is independent and not affiliated with the FDA, CPSC, NHTSA or any government agency. This site may show advertising to cover running costs.
